# REQ01 — Product & Features

**Almost** is a shared Museum of Mistakes. Visitors explore published rooms and exhibits; the demo has two rooms (Math and JavaScript). An exhibit presents a question, an intentionally wrong solution, and choices about where the reasoning failed.

- **Student:** registers or logs in, explores exhibits, chooses a diagnosis, optionally explains their thinking, then sees feedback and the corrected solution. Their first answer is saved; their result stays private.
- **Teacher:** registers or logs in, creates and previews exhibits in Teacher Studio, publishes or archives them, and sees anonymous results for their own exhibits.
- **Account:** registration selects Student or Teacher; login, profile, and password reset are available.

The museum is shared by everyone. Classes, joining a class, student-submitted mistakes, and teacher review queues are outside this demo.
