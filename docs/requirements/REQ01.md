# REQ01 — MVP & Target Learners

## All Most — Museum of Mistakes

### MVP Proposal

* **Subject:** Mathematics
* **Topics:** Fractions + Algebra
* **Target age:** 13–16
* **Interface language:** English
* **Roles:** Student + Teacher
* **Exhibits:** 8–10 common mistakes

### How it works

**Student joins class → submits a mistake → teacher reviews it → teacher publishes it → another student attempts a related problem → reveals the explanation → reflects on the mistake**

### Student

* Join a class
* Explore mistake exhibits
* Submit their own mistake
* Attempt related problems
* Reveal the explanation
* Reflect on what they learned

### Teacher

* Review submitted mistakes
* Add/edit explanations
* Publish approved mistakes as exhibits

### Museum Exhibits

Each exhibit shows a common mistake and explains:

* What went wrong
* Why it is wrong
* How to solve it correctly
* A related problem for students to try

Possible exhibits:

* Adding fractions incorrectly
* Forgetting to simplify fractions
* Distributing a negative incorrectly
* Combining unlike terms
* Moving terms across `=` incorrectly
* Not checking an equation's answer

### Scope

For the MVP, we focus only on **one subject, two topics, two roles, and 8–10 exhibits**.

Anything marked **optional** is only a proposal. It does **not** automatically mean there is a deadline or that someone is assigned to do it.
