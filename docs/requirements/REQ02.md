# REQ02 — Access & Privacy

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Visitor | Browse published rooms and exhibits. |
| Student | Submit one first answer per exhibit version and see only their own result and explanation. |
| Teacher | Create, edit drafts, publish, archive, and view anonymous answer summaries for **their own** exhibits. |

The answer and explanation are hidden until the student submits. Teacher statistics show counts and answer distribution, without student names, emails, IDs, or written reasoning. Teacher access is limited to the selected role and ownership of an exhibit; in this demo anyone may select Teacher during registration, with no identity verification.
